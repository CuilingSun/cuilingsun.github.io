05 / Principles

How I think about AI products

Three ideas I keep coming back to. [Placeholder — Cuiling to refine.]

  1. 01

    The best AI products augment thinking — they don't replace it.

    A product that does the thinking for me is a demo, not a tool. The ones I return to are the ones that make me sharper — better questions, better defaults, faster feedback loops. PaperMind doesn't tell me which papers matter. It just lets me find out faster.

  2. 02

    Models are far from products.

    A model with 0.83 Dice does not mean radiologists will use it. Defaults, latency, where the output lands, what failure looks like — all of that is product. The model is the easy part. The gap between "the model works" and "people use it" is where PMs live.

  3. 03

    Context beats capability.

    In K-Expression I tag every entry with tone, register, and context. The model is GPT-4-class; the value is the scaffolding around it. Capability is a commodity; context is the moat.
